# Godly Inspiration

Browse https://godly.design/ for relevant current examples. Prefer the site's Websites, Apps, Hero, CTA, Footer, or other section matching the task. Open the actual reference or linked product when needed to understand it; a thumbnail alone is weak evidence.

Extract a few concrete decisions that fit the user's brief: hierarchy, composition, typography role, color relationship, imagery, and interaction. Share source links when explaining a reference. Translate these decisions into an original design that uses the user's content, brand, and product constraints. Avoid reproducing another site's layout, copy, logo, or assets.

For a small mechanical UI change, skip inspiration browsing. If the site is unavailable, proceed with the brief and existing design context.
